User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2026 · Meta Platform Compliant

Your Right to Data Erasure: In accordance with global data protection standards and Meta Platform policies, you have the full right to request the permanent deletion of your personal account, social login identifiers, and profile records from Smart Kommunity at any time.

1. How to Delete Your Data from Smart Kommunity

We provide two straightforward methods for users to delete their account and personal data: by personally visiting your subdivision's Homeowners Association (HOA) Administration Office, or by revoking permissions directly through your Facebook account settings.

2. Request Data Deletion in Person at the HOA Office

To protect resident identity and ensure community security within the gated subdivision, property unlinking and user profile deletion are verified and processed in person through your subdivision's official administration:

  1. Visit your subdivision's Homeowners Association (HOA) Administration Office during regular business hours.
  2. Present a valid government-issued ID (e.g., Driver's License, Passport, UMID) and proof of property ownership or tenancy to verify authorization for the associated Block & Lot.
  3. Request the HOA Administrative Officer to perform an Account Disconnection & Data Erasure for your registered mobile number or email account.
  4. The HOA Admin will immediately disconnect your mobile devices, unlink your property access, and queue your user profile for permanent deletion directly through the HOA Management Portal.
  5. Account disconnection takes effect immediately upon administrative verification, with complete database purging finalized within 1 to 3 business days.

3. Instructions for Facebook Login Users (Revoke Access & Request Deletion)

If you registered or signed into Smart Kommunity using your Facebook Login credentials, you can revoke our app's access to your Facebook profile data at any time directly through Facebook's settings:

📘 Steps to Revoke Facebook Access:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account on your web browser or mobile app.
  2. Go to your profile menu and click Settings & Privacy, then select Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar or settings menu, navigate to Your Information or Permissions and select Apps and Websites.
  4. Locate Smart Kommunity under your active applications list.
  5. Click the Remove button next to Smart Kommunity.
  6. Optional: Select the checkbox asking Facebook to delete all historical logs and posts associated with the app, then confirm by clicking Remove.

Once removed from Facebook, our application will immediately lose access to your Facebook token and profile information, preventing any further authentication through that provider.

4. What Happens to Your Data After Deletion?

  • Immediate Effect: Your session tokens are revoked, your mobile device pairings are severed, and you will no longer be able to sign in.
  • Within 30 Days: Your user records (name, email, profile photo, social provider UID, and paired device tokens) are permanently purged from our active central databases.
  • Community Gate Passes: Any active or future scheduled visitor gate passes generated under your account will be immediately cancelled.
  • Encrypted Backups: Residual system backup snapshots are cycled and permanently overwritten within our standard 90-day retention schedule.

5. Statutory & Community Exceptions

In compliance with Philippine corporate laws, tax regulations, and Subdivision Homeowners Association rules, certain financial transaction records (such as issued official receipts, approved Statement of Account payment logs, and historical accounting ledgers) must be retained by your subdivision HOA administration for standard fiscal audit compliance. These records are anonymized where permissible and are never accessible outside authorized accounting personnel.

6. Need Assistance?

If you encounter any issues with your resident account or require assistance unpairing a property, please visit your subdivision's Homeowners Association (HOA) Administration Office during regular office hours to coordinate with your subdivision administrator.

🏢 Subdivision HOA Administration Office

Visit your local subdivision administrative office or clubhouse for in-person identity verification and account management.
