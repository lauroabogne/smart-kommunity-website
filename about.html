What is Smart Kommunity?

Smart Kommunity is an end-to-end community operating system built specifically for residential subdivisions, gated villages, and homeowners associations (HOAs) in the Philippines. By uniting homeowners, administrative boards, and security guardhouses on a single coordinated platform, Smart Kommunity eliminates paperwork, manual gate logs, and billing friction.

Integrated Platform Features

📱 1. Homeowner Portal (Mobile & Progressive Web App)

Designed for daily convenience on Android, iOS, or via browser PWA:

  • Instant Gate Passes: Generate dynamic, high-resolution QR codes for guests, food deliveries, and home maintenance contractors.
  • Transparent Billing (SOA): Real-time dues balance, breakdown of monthly association fees, and seamless 1-tap submission of GCash, Maya, and bank deposit payment receipts.
  • Amenity Reservations: Check real-time calendar availability and reserve subdivision clubhouses, basketball courts, and swimming pools.
  • Incident Reporting: Direct channel to report community concerns (broken streetlamps, garbage issues, noise disturbances) with photo attachments and status tracking.

🛡️ 2. Security Guard Portal

Specialized workstation interface for village entrance gate personnel:

  • Contactless QR Gate Scanner: Millisecond camera verification for incoming guests and deliveries, instantly validating pass status (Active, Expired, or Used).
  • Today's Visitor Directory: Filterable live roster of pre-registered visitors, plate numbers, and hosting resident lots.
  • Shift Management: Shift scheduling logs, supervisor hotlines, and instant incident logging at the guard post.

🖥️ 3. HOA Board & Treasury Web Portal

Enterprise desktop web dashboard for Board Members, Accountants, and Association Managers:

  • Homeowner directory and property title registry with multi-device management.
  • Automated monthly SOA generation and payment proof reconciliation.
  • Broadcast subdivision announcements and push notification dispatches.
  • Comprehensive financial, facility, and security audit logs.

We believe that living in a gated village should be safe, transparent, and connected. Smart Kommunity empowers associations with modern digital tools that elevate property values, bolster perimeter security, and nurture vibrant, peaceful neighborhoods.
