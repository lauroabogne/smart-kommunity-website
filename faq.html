Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using Smart Kommunity for residents, guards, and board officers.

You can sign in immediately using Google Sign-In or Facebook Login with a single tap. Once signed in, your account is authenticated and ready to be paired with your official subdivision lot and block number.
When you sign in for the first time, your profile is authenticated but not yet linked to a specific subdivision property lot. Your subdivision HOA administration will link your registered email to your official Lot and Block record, or provide a pairing code to unlock your property dashboard.
From the Services tab in your app, select Visitor Pass or Delivery Pass. Enter your guest or driver's name, vehicle plate number, and scheduled visit date. The app generates a dynamic QR code that you can send via Messenger or SMS. When your guest arrives at the gate, the security guard simply scans the QR code for instant, contactless clearance.
Open the Billing tab to view your current Statement of Account (SOA). Tap Submit Proof of Payment, choose your payment channel (GCash, Maya, BDO, or BPI), type your transaction reference number, and upload a clear screenshot of your deposit slip or transfer receipt. Once approved by the HOA treasury, your balance updates immediately.
Yes. Smart Kommunity supports multiple authorized resident profiles (Owner, Co-owner, Spouse, Tenant, or Family Member) linked to a single property lot, allowing multiple household members to issue gate passes and view community announcements.
Open our web portal in Google Chrome or Safari on your phone. Tap the browser menu (or share icon in Safari) and choose "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App". This installs Smart Kommunity on your home screen with a native app icon and full-screen experience without needing an app store download.
You can request complete deletion of your account and personal records anytime by personally visiting your subdivision's Homeowners Association (HOA) Administration Office with a valid government ID, or by following the Facebook permission revocation steps on our Data Deletion Instructions page.
