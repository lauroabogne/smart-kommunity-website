Send Feedback

Your feedback directly guides our development roadmap and enhances community life for all residents.

📱 Fastest Way: In-App Feedback

The quickest way to submit feedback or report issues is directly from your mobile app:

Profile → Settings → Help & Feedback

This method automatically includes your app build number and OS version, helping our engineering team reproduce issues faster.

📧 Email Feedback

You can also send your thoughts and ideas directly to our product team:

support.smartkommunity@gmail.com

Helpful details to include for bug reports:

  • A brief description of what occurred
  • Steps to reproduce the error or crash
  • Your device model (e.g., Samsung Galaxy, iPhone, Web browser)
  • Screenshots or screen recordings (if available)

💡 What We'd Love to Hear

  • Feature Ideas: Additional tools your subdivision or HOA needs.
  • Gate Clearance Usability: Suggestions for enhancing QR pass verification speeds.
  • Billing & Statements: Improvements for viewing dues, payments, or official receipts.
  • User Experience: Layout adjustments or accessibility suggestions.

🙏 Maraming Salamat!
