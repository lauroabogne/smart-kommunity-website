Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Compliant with RA 10173 (Philippine DPA) & Meta Platform Policies

🔒 Our Commitment: Smart Kommunity is dedicated to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of homeowners, residents, and community administrators. We strictly enforce multi-tenant database isolation and never sell your personal information to third-party advertisers.

1. Introduction

Smart Kommunity ("we", "our", or "us") provides a specialized mobile application, progressive web app (PWA), and web management portal designed for residential subdivisions, condominiums, and gated communities. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal data when you access or use our platforms.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Resident Information

When you register, pair with a property, or access community services, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Full name, primary email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Property Details: Subdivision phase, block number, lot number, unit number, and residency classification (Owner, Co-owner, Tenant, Resident Member).
  • Vehicle Information: Registered vehicle license plate numbers, vehicle make/model, and RFID/gate sticker identifiers for authorized gate entry.

2.2 Social Sign-In (Google & Facebook)

When you choose to authenticate using third-party social sign-in services such as Google Sign-In or Facebook Login, we access only the basic profile data explicitly authorized by you through the provider's consent screen:

  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier and for receiving critical billing notifications and verification codes.
  • Full / Display Name: Used to personalize your app profile and associate your account with your verified household.
  • Profile Picture URL: Used optionally to display your avatar inside the app interface.
  • Provider User ID (UID): An encrypted token provided by Google or Meta to maintain secure and persistent app sessions.

We do not request, access, or store your Facebook friends list, timeline, private messages, or any social media activity.

2.3 Community Transactions & Operational Records

In the course of using village services, the app records:

  • Digital Gate Passes: Visitor names, vehicle license plates, purpose of visit, entry timestamps, and temporary QR pass tokens.
  • Billing & Payment Submissions: Payment method selected, transaction reference numbers, payment date, and submitted image proofs of payment (receipts/deposit slips).
  • Amenity Reservations: Facility booked (Clubhouse, Basketball Court, Swimming Pool), requested date/time slot, and estimated attendee counts.
  • Incident & Maintenance Reports: Concern category (streetlights, leaks, noise, security), description, urgency level, and user-attached photos.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to deliver and enhance community operations:

  • Authenticating and authorizing resident access to subdivision portals.
  • Generating high-resolution QR passes for contactless gate entry verification by village security personnel.
  • Syncing Statement of Account (SOA) balances and validating payment receipts with your HOA accounting office.
  • Sending emergency alerts, advisory notices, and community announcements via push notifications.
  • Preventing unauthorized access and maintaining digital audit trails for community security.

4. Information Sharing & Tenant Isolation

Smart Kommunity implements strict multi-tenant architectural isolation. Your data is isolated to your designated homeowners association or property management office:

  • Village Security Guards: Gate personnel can view only active visitor QR passes, emergency contacts, and authorized vehicles for entry validation.
  • HOA Board & Administration: Authorized association officers can view billing histories, payment submissions, and incident reports solely for community administration.
  • No Commercial Sale of Data: We will never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketing firms or data brokers.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, encrypted database storage at rest, restricted API Sanctum tokens, and secure cloud infrastructure. Access to administrative controls is restricted to authorized personnel.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

You retain the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. If you move out or no longer reside in the community, you may unlink your property and request account deletion.

To learn more about how to remove your data or revoke Facebook / Google permissions, please review our detailed Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions, comments, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data handling, please coordinate directly with your subdivision's Homeowners Association (HOA) Administration Office during regular business hours to speak with the designated Data Protection Officer or HOA Administrator.

🏢 Subdivision HOA Administration Office

In-person inquiries: Visit your local subdivision administration office or village clubhouse.
